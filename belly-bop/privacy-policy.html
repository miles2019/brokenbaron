Belly Bop: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Belly Bop asks for no name, no e-mail and no account.

Your progress (dishes, stars, Tips, settings) is saved on this device, and "Erase My Progress" in the Setup deletes it at once. Uninstalling the game deletes it too. Only if you sign in to Google Play Games are a few scores sent (see below).

Ads: Belly Bop shows an ad only when you tap a button that says "watch an ad", for example for +3 Bites. These optional rewarded ads come from Google AdMob. To show them, Google may use your device's advertising ID, IP address, rough location and how you interact with the ad, and may use cookies or similar technology. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and the UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time under Setup > Privacy > Ad Privacy Choices. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Games (optional): If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your total stars, your longest Chef's Specials streak and your achievements to Google. Google shows them with your Play Games name and picture on the leaderboards. Nothing else from your progress is sent, and you never have to sign in to play. Google's privacy policy applies to Play Games.

The game plays offline. The internet is only used for these optional ads and, if you signed in, for Google Play Games. The only other permission is vibration for the Rumble option.

There is nothing to buy. There are no lives, no timers that cost money, and no random paid rewards.

Belly Bop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

If anything changes, this notice will be updated inside the game and on the store page before the change goes live.
